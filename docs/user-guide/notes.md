# Notes

Notes are memos attached to [Person](/user-guide/persons) or [Family](/user-guide/families) records. Use them to record pastoral visits, prayer requests, important conversations, or any other relevant information. On a Person record, notes have their own **Notes** tab, sorted newest-first; the tab shows a count badge when the person has notes. On a Family record, notes have their own **Notes** tab too. Both records also have a **Timeline** tab, but it shows activity only — notes are not on it.

---

## Where notes appear

On a Person record, open the **Notes** tab to read, add and edit notes. On a Family record, open the **Notes** tab to read, add and edit notes. The **Timeline** tab on either record gathers the record's activity in one place. Use its filter chips to focus on what matters:

| Filter | Shows |
|--------|-------|
| **Notes** | Notes written by staff |
| **Events** | Calendar events this person/family attended |
| **Emails** | Emails ChurchCRM sent to this person or family — this chip appears only when the record has email entries |
| **Account** | Member Portal account activity — this chip appears only when the record has account activity |
| **System** | Automatic entries: record creation, edits, photo uploads, group changes, deleted-note audit entries |

Long notes show a short preview (about three lines). When a note is clipped, **Read more** appears under it — click it to view the full note in a popup without leaving the page.

## Note Privacy

### Visibility Rules

| Type | Who Can See Full Content | Who Can Edit | Who Can Delete |
|------|--------------------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Public** | Users who can see notes | Creator, and an administrator | Creator or an administrator |
| **Private** | The author, and administrators | The author, and an administrator | Creator or an administrator |

### Private notes

Check **Private** when you save a note.

- The author sees the full note and an **Edit** link.
- An administrator sees the full private note and an **Edit** link, even when someone else wrote it.
- Other users do not see the note at all. There is no placeholder such as "[Private Note — visible only to creator]".

Use a private note for pastoral details you do not want the rest of the staff to read.

---

## Deleting a Note

Deleting a note creates a **System** timeline entry recording who deleted it and whose note it was. This keeps the audit trail intact without exposing the deleted content.

> **Warning:** Deleted note content is not recoverable.

---

## Adding a Note

1. Open the Person or Family record
2. Click **Add Note** in the page header — a popup editor opens on the record page
3. Enter your note — the popup is a rich-text editor, so you can format the text as you write
4. Check **Private** if only you should see this note
5. Click **Save**

---

## Editing a Note

The author can edit the note. An administrator can edit it as well, including a private note written by someone else. Other users cannot.

1. Find the note on the **Notes** tab (Person or Family record)
2. Click **Edit** in the note's footer — the same popup editor opens with the note loaded
3. Make your changes and click **Save**

> **Tip:** To make a private note public, edit it and uncheck **Private**.

---

## Deleting a Note

1. Find the note on the **Notes** tab (Person or Family record)
2. Click **Delete** in the note's footer
3. Confirm in the dialog

Only the note's author or an Admin can delete a note.
