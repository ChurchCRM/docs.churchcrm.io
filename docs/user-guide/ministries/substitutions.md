# Substitutions

A volunteer who is on for a date (whether they have accepted it or not answered yet) and then cannot make it does not simply drop out: they propose a **substitute** who has already agreed, and a coordinator or team leader decides.

## How it works

1. **The volunteer proposes.** From their schedule in the Member Portal they choose **Find a sub**, pick someone qualified for the same position (never themselves, never someone who already holds that position on that date), add a note, and send it. Their card shows a *"Substitute asked: …"* badge and *"Waiting for your coordinator to confirm."*
2. **Until it is decided, the original volunteer is still expected.** Nothing changes on the occurrence; their row keeps its status.
3. **The coordinator decides.** The request appears at the bottom of the [staffing view](./staffing-an-occurrence.md) (position, volunteer, proposed substitute and when it was proposed) and, when the date falls in the dashboard's window, under **Substitution requests** on the [Ministry Dashboard](./ministry-dashboard.md), with the volunteer's note.
   - **Approve** — the original volunteer is released and the substitute takes the position; both are told. The original row becomes **Substituted** (its history is kept) and an **Accepted** row for the substitute appears on the same position. The position still counts as filled once, not twice.
   - **Reject** — the original volunteer keeps the position; both are told.
4. **Both people are emailed** with the decision: *"Your substitute request was approved"* or the *"was not approved"* version, which says the original volunteer is still expected.

## Withdrawing

Only the volunteer who proposed a substitute can take it back (**Take that back** on their schedule card), as long as it has not been decided. A coordinator approves or rejects; they cannot withdraw on someone else's behalf.

## Who may decide

Anyone who may staff the occurrence: an administrator, a Manage Ministries user, the ministry's coordinators, or the leader of the team the schedule belongs to. See [Who can do what](./permissions.md).
